**Snooze Button Roast** — exactly one line, at most 200 characters, roasting an alarm clock that rings at 6 AM on a Saturday. The punchline lands in the last few words: "Six AM on a Saturday? Even the sun sleeps in — you are the only thing in this house with a worse social life than me."

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (standard library only, no network) writes the roast to `/output/entry.txt` and checks it against the gate (1–200 chars).
